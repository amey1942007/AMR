AMR4 is in development. Navigation and mapping results will be published once they're validated.

1Overview

AMR4 rolls on four mecanum wheels, carries an RPLidar A1 on USB and runs ROS 2 Humble on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Design, not results

AMR4 is described as built so far. No mapping or navigation results are claimed yet.

Custom driver

A custom motor driver runs the four mecanum wheels.

Mecanum odometry

Wheel odometry written for mecanum kinematics tracks how the robot moves.
