Hardware Bot

A compact differential-drive robot that maps rooms with lidar, navigates on its own, and labels what it sees.

1Overview

Hardware Bot drives on two DDSM115 hub motors with casters for balance. A Raspberry Pi 5 runs ROS 2 Jazzy; an RPLidar S2E on Ethernet scans the room, and a pan/tilt camera looks for objects.
