1Who we are

AMR ("we") runs amrslam.tech. Contact: amey@amrslam.tech. Legal entity name and address: pending.

2What we collect

Contact form: your name, email, optional company, topic and message, plus the page URL and your browser user-agent.

Emails you send us.

No accounts and no tracking cookies. The site sets no analytics cookies.

3Why we use it (legal basis)

To reply to your enquiry, on the basis of consent or legitimate interest (to be confirmed under India's DPDP Act 2023 and, for EU visitors, the GDPR).

4Where it's stored

Supabase (database) and Zoho Mail (email). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

5How long

Form messages are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

6Sharing

We do not sell or share your data, except with the providers above that process it for us, or when the law requires it.

7Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent: email amey@amrslam.tech and we will reply within 30 days. Grievance officer (DPDP): pending.

8Security

HTTPS everywhere, database access rules that only let the public add messages, and no secret keys in the browser.

9Children

This site is not directed at children under 18.

10Changes

We will update this page and the date above when the policy changes.
